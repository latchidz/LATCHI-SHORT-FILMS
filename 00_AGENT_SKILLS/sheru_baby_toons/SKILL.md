A baby lion with a mighty heart — and a roar that comes out as a tiny squeak.

**What it is:**
A kids cartoon skill built around Sheru, a baby lion cub with a soft round mane, a very serious practice face and a tail that puffs up when he is trying to be brave, who lives in a sunny jungle clearing with his gentle animal friends. Every film is one roar moment: Sheru takes a deep breath, plants his feet, opens wide — "squeak!" — a tiny cute squeak. His friends pretend to be terribly scared, paws up, tumbling over backwards, and the clearing giggles. Then the squeak does something wonderful — it is the only sound gentle enough, small enough or funny enough to save the day — and everyone celebrates the smallest big sound in the jungle.

**What you get:**
1. One complete 20–30 second cartoon film plan, built from 2–3 joined 10-second beats
2. Episode script — very simple lines, breath-in build-ups kids can join, the squeak as a repeat-along sound gag, friends' pretend-scared lines, one laugh every few seconds
3. Storyboards — beat-by-beat shots, vertical 9:16 framing, bright sunny jungle colours, no dark or scary jungle
4. Character sheets — Sheru (round soft mane, puff-tail when brave) and the jungle friends look the same in every episode
5. Location plates — sunny clearing, big flat rock, watering hole, flower meadow, cosy rainy shelter (never scary)
6. A warm ending — one line about every voice being useful, or everyone squeaking together

**Pipeline:**
1. Intake — one moment where a mighty roar is expected
2. Build-up design — Sheru's all-day practice, the deep breath, the planted feet — the funnier the seriousness, the better the squeak lands
3. Pretend-scared beat — the whole jungle kindly pretends to tremble and topple at the tiny squeak, pure giggles, never teasing
4. Squeak-saves-the-day beat — the one thing only the tiny squeak can do in this story (calm, call, warn softly, or make a grumpy moment funny)
5. Script — short simple lines kids can repeat, kind giggles only, Sheru is never teased or shamed
6. Storyboard — 2–3 beats joined into one 20–30 second film (the build-up, the squeak and pretend-scared giggles, the saving ending)
7. Clip render — bright cartoon style, same characters throughout, mane and puff-tail never change
8. Self-check — age-safe, no hunting / fighting / prey scenes, no scary moments, courage is honoured, small voices are celebrated, ending always warm

**Modes:**
1. Full auto-run — one roar moment in, full cartoon plan out
2. Practice mode — Sheru trains all day with funny roar teachers (a frog, a peacock) and the squeak stays a squeak, but it gets better at everything except roaring
3. Pretend-scared mode — extra focus on the jungle friends' silly pretend-trembling and toppling whenever Sheru squeaks
4. Squeak-rescue mode — the tiny squeak saves the day: it calms a baby animal a loud roar would frighten, or slips through a small crack to call friends
5. Sequel — Sheru's next roar moment in the same sunny jungle

**Example prompts:**
1. "Make a 25-second cartoon: Sheru climbs the big flat rock to greet the morning with his mightiest roar, out comes a tiny squeak — the whole jungle pretends to be scared and topples over giggling, and his squeak wakes the sleepy sunflowers one by one."
2. "A baby elephant is stuck behind fallen branches and cries at loud sounds — Sheru's soft little squeak calms it while friends quietly clear the way, and the jungle pretends to faint at his 'mighty roar'. 20 seconds, gentle and brave."
3. "Roar practice day: frog, peacock and parrot each teach Sheru their loudest call; his roar stays a squeak, but by evening the whole jungle is squeaking his tune and pretending to be scared together. 25 seconds."
4. "A friend's baby brother has wandered to the watering hole and only Sheru's squeak — the tiny sound the baby loves — brings him toddling back happily while everyone pretends to tremble. 20 seconds."

**When to use:** kids cartoon channels and family apps, brave-heart gentle stories, sound-play episodes, "small is special" endings, 20–30 second format.
**Not the right tool when:** you want hunting, fighting, scary predators, anyone mocked for a small voice, or content for adults. In this skill Sheru is brave first and funny second, and his squeak is a gift, not a flaw.
