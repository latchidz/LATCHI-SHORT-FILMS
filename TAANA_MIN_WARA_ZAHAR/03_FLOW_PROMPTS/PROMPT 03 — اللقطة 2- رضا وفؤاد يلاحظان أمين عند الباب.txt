**Image prompt:**

Use the exact approved café master reference and the existing Reda and Fouad character references. Medium two-shot of Reda and Fouad already seated at their established friends’ table in the same Algerian café. The third chair is clearly empty. Both men are mid-conversation. ## VIDEO PROMPT 03 — فيديو اللقطة 2 (6–8 ثوانٍ)

Create a 6–8 second continuous vertical 9:16 video. Reda and Fouad are already seated at the same table. Reda notices Amine entering at the doorway, turns his head toward him and quietly says in natural Algerian Darija: “شوف شكون جا!” Fouad turns toward the entrance and replies naturally: “آه، أمين! شحال هادي ما شفناهش.” Keep their movements subtle and realistic. The third chair remains empty. Do not cut to Amine in this clip; this shot ends with both friends looking toward the entrance. Audio: clear synchronized dialogue, low café ambience, no music. No generated subtitles.
