**Image prompt:**

Use the same café master reference and exact Reda and Fouad character references. Medium two-shot at the same friends’ table. Reda and Fouad are alone; Amine’s chair is empty in its established position. Reda leans slightly closer and speaks quietly, while Fouad listens with a conflicted but complicit expression. The same cups remain where they were. The elderly man may be a soft-focus shape near the windows in the background, consistent with his established table. Same daylight, same architecture, same outfits, vertical 9:16, realistic cinematic style, no text.

**Negative prompt:** Amine sitting at table, friends standing, elderly man joining them, moved furniture, changed room, loud laughing, exaggerated villain expressions, text, logos, extra cups.

## VIDEO PROMPT 08 — فيديو اللقطة 7 (8–10 ثوانٍ)

Create an 8–10 second continuous vertical 9:16 medium two-shot. Reda checks that Amine is away, lowers his voice and says in Algerian Darija: “تعرف أمين؟ ديما يدير روحو فاهم كلش، بصح…” Fouad glances toward the direction Amine walked, then replies quietly: “صح، وحتى كي يعاون الناس، يبقى يحكي عليها كأنه هو اللي دار كلش.” Their behavior should feel like real friends gossiping, not cartoon villains. Amine’s chair stays empty; the elderly man is only subtly visible in the background. Natural lip sync, low café ambience, no music, no subtitles.
