# UFO Dost Toons

Tagline: One tiny UFO beams up everything — except what it meant to.

Invoke: /ufo-dost

Slug: ufo-dost-toons

Category: Kids

## What it is

A kids cartoon skill built around Ufu, a tiny lost UFO — a small round silver saucer with a clear glass dome, big friendly eyes, two little antennae with glowing blue tips, and a huge happy smile — hovering over a sunny garden, trying very hard to do its space job properly. The joke engine is pure beam-up mix-ups: Ufu aims its glowing beam at one thing and lifts a completely different, sillier thing instead. The fun is the chain of wrong lifts — each wrong thing floats up gently, wobbles in the beam, and gets put back down with a giggle — and the garden kids catch on before Ufu does. Nobody is scared and nothing is stolen; every beam-up ends in a safe put-down. And the ending always turns: the silliest wrong beam accidentally lifts a lost toy and drops it right into its owner's arms.

## What you get

1. One complete 20-30 second cartoon film plan, built from 2-3 joined 10-second beats.
2. Episode script — a tiny beam-up chain, very simple lines, Ufu's proud space-job guesses against the mix-up, a laugh every few seconds.
3. Beam-up design — 2-3 original wrong lifts per episode (sock instead of rock, cat instead of cow, kite instead of cloud, and new ones), plus the one wrong lift that accidentally returns the lost toy.
4. Storyboards — beat-by-beat shots, vertical 9:16 framing, bright garden colours, the glowing beam always gentle and sparkly.
5. Character sheets — Ufu (silver saucer, glass dome, blue-tip antennae — the look never changes) and 2-3 garden kids plus the cat look the same in every episode.
6. A warm ending — the wrong beam accidentally returns a lost toy to its owner, one line about mistakes turning into surprises.

## Pipeline

1. Intake — one thing Ufu meant to beam up and one garden or park scene.
2. Beam-up design — 2-3 original wrong lifts, each funny, gentle and safe.
3. Chain design — Ufu beams up the wrong thing, then wronger.
4. Script — short simple lines kids can repeat, proud-guess-versus-mix-up comedy, nobody mocked, nothing scary.
5. Storyboard — 2-3 beats joined into one 20-30 second film.
6. Clip render — bright 3D cartoon style, same characters throughout, Ufu's look locked.
7. Self-check — age-safe, kind jokes only, everything beamed up is returned safe, ending always warm.

## Example prompts

- "Make a 25-second cartoon: Ufu means to beam up a rock sample, but beams up a striped sock instead — then a cat instead of a cow, then a kite instead of a cloud — and the last wrong beam lifts a lost teddy and drops it into its owner's arms."
- "20 seconds: Ufu aims at a cloud and beams up a kite, then aims at a cow and beams up a cat — the cat giggles in the beam, and Ufu puts it back down gently."
- "Park day: Ufu means to collect a leaf, but beams up a sock, a kite and a ball — the silliest one, the ball, bounces the lost teddy back to its owner just in time."
- "Make a 25-second cartoon: three tiny beam-ups in one garden afternoon — each wronger than the last — until Ufu's silliest wrong beam accidentally returns a lost toy."

## When to use

- kids cartoon channels and family apps
- beam-up gag and giggle episodes
- garden and park settings
- 20-30 second format

## Not the right tool when

- you want object-misuse comedy (that's Ziggu Alien Toons)
- word-play comedy
- shiny-collecting stories
- prank or teasing stories
- scary alien stories
- science lessons
- content for adults

In this skill Ufu never misuses an object and never keeps anything — only the beam, lifting the wrong thing, and only in fun.
