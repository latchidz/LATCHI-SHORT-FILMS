# Gilhari Toons

One busy squirrel who hides her snacks in the best places — and forgets them all.

**What it is:**
A kids cartoon skill built around Chutki, a small rust-orange squirrel with three dark tail stripes, very fast feet and cheeks that puff up when she is thinking, who lives in a park neem tree. Every film is one hide-and-search: Chutki proudly hides nuts ("iss baar toh pakka yaad rahega!"), a day or a season passes, hunger strikes, and the great search begins — flowerpot, old shoe, statue's hat — each spot giving a silly surprise instead of a nut, until the friends' laughter and one cheek-nut save the snack time, and Chutki shares whatever is found.

**What you get:**
1. One complete 20–30 second cartoon film plan, built from 2–3 joined 10-second beats
2. Episode script — very simple lines, Chutki's confident hiding lines against her blank search face, one laugh every few seconds
3. Storyboards — beat-by-beat shots, vertical 9:16 framing, bright park colours
4. Character sheets — Chutki (three tail stripes, puffy thinking cheeks) and the park friends look the same in every episode
5. Location plates — neem tree hollow, flowerpots row, park bench, statue corner, rainy-day shelter
6. A warm ending — whatever is found is shared, and one line about friends helping us remember

**Pipeline:**
1. Intake — a hiding day, a season change, or a hungry search moment
2. Hiding design — three clever hiding spots, each funnier and more "unforgettable" than the last
3. Search design — the order of wrong guesses and the funny surprise inside each spot
4. Script — short simple lines kids can repeat, confident-versus-blank comedy, nobody mocks Chutki's forgetting
5. Storyboard — 2–3 beats joined into one 20–30 second film (the proud hiding, the great search, the cheek-nut ending)
6. Clip render — bright cartoon style, same characters throughout, tail stripes and cheek puffs never change
7. Self-check — age-safe, no stealing (all nuts are Chutki's own), no greed lesson hammered in, found food is always shared, ending always warm

**Modes:**
1. Full auto-run — one hiding idea in, full cartoon plan out
2. Treasure-hunt mode — the whole film is the search, map drawn by a friend from Chutki's half-memories
3. Season mode — hide in autumn, search in winter; the park looks different and every spot is a surprise
4. Picnic mode — Chutki hides the picnic snacks "safely" and the friends must find lunch itself
5. Sequel — Chutki's next hiding day in the same park, new spots

**Example prompts:**
1. "Make a 25-second cartoon: Chutki hides three nuts — flowerpot, old shoe, statue's hat — says she will never forget, and next day remembers none of them; the search finds a sleeping snail, a lost ball and finally the nut in her own cheek."
2. "Autumn to winter: Chutki buries nuts all over the park in autumn; in snow-white winter she and her friends dig at all the wrong white bumps. 20 seconds, cosy shared ending."
3. "Picnic rescue: Chutki hid the picnic groundnuts 'in the safest place ever' — the friends follow her wobbly memory map and find lunch under her own picnic mat. 25 seconds."
4. "A memory-game episode: Chutki ties a ribbon on the tree for every hiding spot, then follows the ribbons in the funniest wrong order. 20 seconds."

**When to use:** kids cartoon channels and family apps, treasure-hunt and memory-game stories, park and season episodes, sharing endings, 20–30 second format.
**Not the right tool when:** you want stealing, hoarding or greed morals, anyone teased for forgetting, or content for adults. In this skill forgetting is funny and human, friends help, and every find is shared.
