## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked tripod camera. Over ten seconds the cut is bridged as a slow, almost
imperceptible arc that swings around the desk from the wide doorway framing to the
reverse angle over the doctor's shoulder. Nothing snaps; the room simply rotates
around the two men.

THE DOCTOR speaks calmly, his head lowered to the file, his lips moving in a slow
measured rhythm, his right hand tapping one finger once on the paper. He never lifts
his eyes to the lens.

THE FATHER sits still. His hands stay flat on the desk edge. At second four his
throat moves in a single swallow; at second seven his fingers curl inward against
the wood, then relax again. HIS HEAD TURNS LAST, toward the doctor, and HIS EYES
SETTLE ON THE DOCTOR'S EYES - everything else on his face stays neutral and closed.

MARYAM, on the near chair, turns her head slowly toward her father and lifts one
small hand, then lets it fall back into her lap without touching him. Her eyes go
to his cheek and stay there.

DUST turns slowly in the shaft of window light. The paper stack on the desk lifts a
corner and settles.
```
