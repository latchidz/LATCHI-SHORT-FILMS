## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera deep in the shop. This is one continuous approach.

SECONDS 0-3: the glass door swings and AMIN STEPS IN. He holds the handle for a beat,
lets it go, and starts down the aisle. The blue dusk is behind him; the amber shop is
ahead. He does not look up. The medical folder swings once against his thigh.

SECONDS 3-7: he walks the aisle toward us. The camera does not follow him - he grows in
the frame instead. Near the counter he stops, and only then does he lift his head.

SECONDS 7-10: the framing settles on the two men across the counter. YOUSSEF, behind it,
sunglasses on, lifts one hand an inch off the wood and puts it back down. AMIN's mouth
opens on the first word of the sentence and stays open at the end of the shot - he is
about to speak when we cut.

NEITHER MAN LOOKS AT THE LENS. Not once.
```
