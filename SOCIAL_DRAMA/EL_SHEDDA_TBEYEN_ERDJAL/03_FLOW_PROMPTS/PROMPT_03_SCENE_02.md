## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera, low, tracking him as he crosses the frame - but he slows, so the frame
holds him.

HE WALKS FAST for the first four seconds. Then his pace breaks - three quick steps,
a stumble that he turns into a normal step, a hand pressed briefly against a shuttered
shop front to steady himself, and he keeps going. He never looks back and he never
looks at the lens; his eyes stay on the pavement ahead of him.

In the last three seconds he reaches the perfume shop, transfers the folder from his
arm to his left hand, and puts his right hand on the brass handle. The door gives a
few centimetres and warm amber light runs out across the wet paving stones.

DUST and paper scraps drift across the road. A plastic bag lifts and drops in the gutter.
```
