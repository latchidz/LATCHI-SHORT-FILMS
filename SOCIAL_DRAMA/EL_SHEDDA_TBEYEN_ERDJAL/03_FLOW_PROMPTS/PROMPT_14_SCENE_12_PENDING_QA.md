## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera at the end of the row. Almost nothing moves. That is the point.

THE MEN ARE ASLEEP OR NEAR IT. Hamza's head is against the wall. Karim's forehead rests
on his fists. Rami stares at the floor between his boots. Youssef sits upright and turns
his head slowly to look at Amin.

AMIN'S HEAD IS BACK, EYES CLOSED, THE ENVELOPE FLAT ON HIS KNEES.

YOUSSEF'S HAND COMES ACROSS AND RESTS on Amin's forearm. It does not grip. It just stays.

THE CAMERA MOVES IN SLOWLY and the frame settles on the hands and the paper - and holds
there while the clock ticks.

THE SINGLE TUBE stays lit. Nothing else happens. Nobody looks at the lens.
```
