## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera. The frame opens by a step - the two men stay exactly where they are
while the rest of the room comes in around them.

THE EMBRACE IS ALREADY HAPPENING. Youssef pulls Amin in hard. His right hand, still
holding the envelope, presses it flat against Amin's back. His other hand goes to the
back of Amin's head and holds it there.

AMIN'S FACE STAYS BURIED in Youssef's shoulder the whole time. IT IS NEVER VISIBLE.

RAMI comes in from the left and puts one heavy hand on both their shoulders. KARIM steps
back half a pace to give them room and looks at his own shoes. HAMZA stays by the chairs,
turns his head toward the wall, and wipes his face with the back of his hand.

THE GROUP DOES NOT POSE. They breathe. The lino floor takes the light.
```
