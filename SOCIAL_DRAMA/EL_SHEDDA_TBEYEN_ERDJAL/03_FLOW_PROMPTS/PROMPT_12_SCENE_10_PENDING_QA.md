## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked camera, square to the side of both men. They never turn to us.

YOUSSEF SPEAKS. His hands do not move while he speaks - the envelope stays out in the air
between them, held level, both hands.

AMIN DOES NOT TAKE IT. For four seconds he just looks at it, his arms at his sides, the
medical folder clamped under his left elbow.

THEN HE REACHES. HIS LEFT HAND CLOSES AROUND THE ENVELOPE, and Youssef's right hand lets
go at the same moment - one hand off, the other on, the paper transferring without a
pause.

AS THE HAND CLOSES, THE CAMERA MOVES IN and the frame drops to the hands: the envelope
creases very slightly in the middle between the four fingers. The paper is sealed. Nothing
falls out.

NO FACE IS IN THE LAST FRAME. The camera ends on the hands and the paper.
```
