## 🎥 MOTION PROMPT — النص اللي تلصقو في Flow

```
Locked tripod camera. The shot does not move. The FOCUS racks - slowly, on its own -
downward off the father's face, across the desk, and settles on the wood in the last
four seconds where the hands are.

THE FATHER holds still for the first three seconds, eyes still on the doctor. Then he
lowers his head and his shoulders drop about a centimetre. His right hand slides
across the wood toward the small hand resting there, and CLOSES OVER IT. His thumb
presses softly into her palm, twice, like a slow heartbeat.

MARYAM's fingers unfold and curl back around his.

IN THE SOFT BACKGROUND the doctor takes off his glasses and rubs his eyes with the
heel of one hand.

No one looks at the lens. The camera is not in the room.
```
