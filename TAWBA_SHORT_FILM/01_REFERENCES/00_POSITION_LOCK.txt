B) PRAYER HALL  (SC08 - SC10)   master = MASTER_PRAYER_STANDING.png
--------------------------------------------------------------------
CAMERA
  In FRONT of the two men, facing them, ~1.2 m above the carpet,
  ~3 m away, slightly off-axis. Both fully in frame, head to feet,
  filling the lower two thirds of the 9:16 frame.

STANCE — they stand as one row
  - Both BAREFOOT.
  - Same cream carpet stripe, exactly the same line.
  - Upper arms and shoulders TOUCHING, no gap.
  - Heels touching, toes touching, inner edges of the feet in contact.
  - Same shoulder height, same spine, same head angle. Neither ahead.

HANDS
  Right forearm folded across the body, left forearm resting over it —
  arms simply crossed and folded over each other, hands relaxed and
  partly open.  NOT palm-on-palm.  NOT hand pressed on the back of
  the other hand.  No clasp, no interlocked fingers.

GAZE
  Both look down at the place of prostration. NEVER at the lens.

  plain whitewashed walls, two or three plain white columns, the green
  carpet with its cream stripes running back empty, warm pendant lamps
  on chains, plain high ceiling, cool dawn light, faint dust.
  NO mihrab. NO minbar. NO books. NO shelf. NO table. NO chairs.
  NO clock. NO notice board. NO speaker. NO beads. NO furniture.
  NO calligraphy. NO lettering. NO objects of any kind.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
CAMERA
  In FRONT of the two men, ~1.1 m above the floor, ~2.5 m away,
  slightly left of centre. Tiled wall recedes along the LEFT of frame.

SEATING — identical, side by side
  - Same low stone bench, upper arms and shoulders TOUCHING, no gap.
  - Hips level, same height, neither leaner forward than the other.
  - Both BAREFOOT, feet side by side and level on the wet stone.
  - Both sleeves pushed ABOVE the elbows, at the same point.

ACTION
  Both perform the SAME step at the SAME moment, mirrored:
  stage 1 = washing both hands under the water (MASTER_WUDU_TOGETHER)
  stage 2 = Youcef speaks, turns his head to Amin; Amin turns to him

  NO soap. NO bottles. NO buckets. NO towels. NO stools. NO shelves.
