TAWBA — VIDEO PROMPTS (Google Flow)
===================================

FOR EVERY CLIP
1. Open 02_SCENES/ and pick the stills named in that clip's FRAMES line.
2. Upload FIRST FRAME, and LAST FRAME where the clip has one.
3. Paste the matching VIDEO PROMPT file exactly as it is.
4. 10 seconds, 9:16 (1080x1920), 30 fps.

HARD RULES (same in every clip)
- Locked-off tripod. At most a 3% push-in. No pan, no handheld shake, no cut inside a clip.
- Live-action realism only. No 3D, no illustration, no CGI look.
- Darja algérienne, urban Algerian accent, native Algerian voices.
  Quranic and prophetic phrases in clear classical Arabic.
  Never Egyptian, Gulf or Syrian. Pronounce the words exactly as written.
- Diegetic sound only: wind, water, footsteps, cloth, breath, room tone.
  NO MUSIC OF ANY KIND, no score, no humming, no choir.
- No subtitles, no burned-in text, no logos, no watermark.
- Restrained performance. Real blinks, believable breathing, correct hands.
