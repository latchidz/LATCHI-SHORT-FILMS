# حكايات أمين — الحلقة 1 — نظام البرومبتات الاحترافي V2.1

> **الفلسفة:** كل برومبت **كامل ومستقل بذاته** (يُنسخ كما هو)، لكنه مبني حرفياً من نفس الكتل الثابتة تحت — لهذا **الكراج والشارع والرصفة والضوء ومواقف السيارات لا تتغير أبداً بين اللقطات**. الذي يتغير فقط: الحركة + الشخصيات + زاوية الكاميرا المعلنة.
> **القاعدة الذهبية:** كل برومبت مشهد = `[STYLE]` ثم `[GARAGE]` ثم `[STAGING]` ثم `ACTION` المشهد، مع إرفاق صور المراجع المذكورة.

---

## الكتلة [STYLE] — الأسلوب (ثابتة في كل برومبت)
```
Photorealistic cinematic film still from a real Algerian TV drama, live-action look, 16:9 landscape. Natural morning light, realistic skin texture, realistic hands and anatomy. Characters always look at what they interact with, never at the camera. Vehicles at TRUE realistic scale and distance, never oversized. No text, no logos, no watermark, no extra people, no duplicated limbs, no 3D render look, no cartoon.
```

## الكتلة [GARAGE] — المرجع الرئيسي للكراج (مقفلة — كلمة بكلمة في كل لقطة)
```
THE GARAGE — locked geography, identical in every single shot: an old worn concrete building on a street corner in a working-class Algerian neighborhood. The main facade has TWO adjacent huge bays, BOTH WIDE OPEN — their rusty blue-grey metal doors fully raised, both interiors fully visible. Inside the two bays there is ONLY mechanic tools and equipment — a red tool cabinet, a workbench, wall-mounted tools — and ONE blue vintage Renault 4 raised on a blue two-post hydraulic lift in the right bay. No other cars, no barrels, no clutter. Above the left bay runs a row of rectangular glass transom windows. A tall concrete utility pole stands exactly at the LEFT corner of the building, where the street branches toward the background left between old houses with faded light-yellow and white paint and electric wires crossing the sky. In front of the garage: a cracked asphalt apron with dark oil stains. Morning sunlight comes from the street side, identical in every shot. Never mirror the building, never change the street layout, never add or remove bays.
```

## الكتلة [STAGING] — المواقع المقفلة (استمرارية بين كل اللقطات)
```
STAGING — locked across all shots: the orange flatbed tow truck always parks on the STREET at the LEFT, beside the concrete utility pole. The customer's black luxury sedan always parks on the cracked asphalt apron DIRECTLY IN FRONT OF THE RIGHT OPEN BAY, nose pointing LEFT, driver side toward the camera. The slate-grey Dacia Sandero Stepway later parks at this exact same apron spot, nose LEFT. The garage, the street, the light and the parking positions NEVER change between shots — only the action and the announced camera angle change.
```
